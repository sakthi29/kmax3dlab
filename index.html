5 · YouTube intro (6 seconds)

The logo is “printed” layer by layer, then the name and tagline appear. Record the player with your phone or screen recorder to use it as your video intro.

Storyboard & voice-over
  1. 0.0–2.7s: scan line prints the logo from bottom to top (soft printer hum).
  2. 2.8s: logo pulses, name slides in.
  3. 3.8s: tagline fades in. Voice: “Welcome to .”
  4. 5–6s: hold, then cut to your first shot. Add end text: “Subscribe for new prints every week.”
